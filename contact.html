FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long do Kadha perfumes last?

Our Eau de Parfums typically last 8–12 hours on skin, and our Eau de Toilettes 4–6 hours. Longevity varies with skin type and climate.

Can I try a perfume before buying a full bottle?

Yes. Every perfume comes in a 10 ml size, and every order includes a complimentary sample of another fragrance.

How long does shipping take?

Orders ship within 1–2 working days and arrive in 3–7 days across India. Shipping is free on orders above ₹1,999.

What is your return policy?

Unopened bottles can be returned within 7 days of delivery for a full refund. For hygiene reasons we can't accept opened bottles.

How should I store my perfume?

Keep it away from direct sunlight and heat, ideally in its box in a cool, dry place. Avoid storing it in the bathroom.

Are your perfumes cruelty-free?

Yes. None of our products or ingredients are tested on animals.
