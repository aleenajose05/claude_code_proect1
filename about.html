Our story

Kadha means story.

We believe a fragrance is never just a smell. It's the rain on the drive home, the shawl that still holds someone's warmth, the festival evening you'll remember for years.

Born from memories

Every Kadha perfume starts with a story written before a single drop is blended — a place, a person or a feeling. Only then do our perfumers go looking for the notes that tell it.

Rooted in nature

From monsoon-soaked vetiver to sun-dried citrus and wild moss, we draw on natural ingredients and the landscapes that inspire them, sourced responsibly and blended in small batches.

Made to last

Our Eau de Parfums carry high concentrations of perfume oil, so your story stays with you from morning to night — and lingers in the room after you've left.
